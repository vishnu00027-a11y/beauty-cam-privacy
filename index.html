Children's Privacy

Beauty Cam Plus is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

The application may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, payments, or other app functionality. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Contact
